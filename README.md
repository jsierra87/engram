## ❓ Troubleshooting common issues

**Installer does not run:**  
Right-click the `.exe` file and select **Run as administrator**. Check your antivirus software—it may block new apps.

**Unable to connect AI accounts:**  
Double-check your API keys or login details. Visit the AI provider’s site to confirm permissions.

**Memory not syncing:**  
Make sure your internet is active. Restart engram or your computer to refresh connections.

**App crashes or freezes:**  
Close unused apps to free memory. Reinstall engram if problems persist.
